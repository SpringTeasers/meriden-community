About this site

This is an independent community guide to Meriden, Connecticut. It is not an official City of Meriden website, and it cannot process city business. For anything official — permits, records, payments, reports — use the city's own site at meridenct.gov (opens in a new tab) or call City Hall on (203) 630-4000.

There is no contact form on this page: a static site cannot receive form submissions, and a form that silently discarded your message would be worse than none. Every route above reaches the right office directly.
