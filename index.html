A city between two cities

Meriden sits in New Haven County, halfway between New Haven and Hartford — close enough to both to be connected to their economies and culture, and distinct enough to have kept its own centre, parks and character.

Meriden is known as The Silver City, a nickname earned from the silver and manufacturing industry that built the city. It was settled in 1661 and became a town in 1806.

If you are new here, start with About Meriden for the history and the key facts. If you are visiting, start with Things to Do — Hubbard Park, Castle Craig and the Daffodil Festival are the places people ask about first.

Need to reach the city?

City Hall is at 142 East Main Street, Meriden, CT 06450. Call (203) 630-4000 for general enquiries. The Police Department's non-emergency line is 203-238-1911, and the Utilities Office is on 203-630-4256.

Emergency calls should always go to 911.
